What it changes

Small controls, kept out of the way

01

Archive links

Each normal result gets links to its latest Wayback Machine copy, archive.today and the Internet Archive save page.

02

More results in place

The button requests one Google results page at a time. It stops near 100, on a captcha, or when Google returns no more usable results.

Local by default

No analytics and no account

Your two preferences stay in Chrome's local extension storage. Search Restore contacts Google only after you press its load button. Archive sites open only after you choose one of their links.
